Privacy Policy

Draft document only; not final legal language and pending review.

Basic principles

The project aims to minimize collection of personal data by default. Data may be collected only where necessary to improve site functionality, support access, analytics, or service operation.

Wallet addresses, browser metadata, IP addresses, and access logs may be processed in a privacy-respecting manner where enabled by site technology or hosting provider.

Users should not provide seed phrases, private keys, or secret recovery data through the website or any support channel.

Cookies and analytics

Cookies may be used for essential site functionality, analytics, and session memory. A site owner should clearly disclose cookie use and provide an opt-out where applicable under the legal jurisdiction in force.

Any analytics package should favor privacy-respecting measurement and avoid excessive personal data collection.
